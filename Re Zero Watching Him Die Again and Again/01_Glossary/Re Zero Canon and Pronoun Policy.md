# Re:Zero English-to-Thai Canon and Pronoun Policy

This is a compact setup policy, not a general fantasy glossary. Add a new
entry only when a term recurs, carries canon meaning, or is needed to prevent
name or relationship drift.

## Canon spelling

- Use the approved Thai spellings in the individual seed notes.
- Keep character names as names; do not translate or paraphrase them.
- Prefer the common Thai forms for Re:Zero names and institutions. If a later
  source passage proves a different official/common form, stop and review the
  seed rather than silently creating a second spelling.
- Keep recurring canon concepts such as Return by Death, Witch Factor, Witch
  Cult, Sin Archbishop, Lugunica, and Sanctuary stable.
- Do not approve generic words such as death, magic, knight, mansion, or
  spirit unless the source uses them as a recurring named concept.

## Pronouns and address

- Subaru's default first-person voice is ผม. Preserve a stronger or more
  intimate register only when the English context clearly supports it.
- Choose Thai forms of you and address terms from relationship, age, rank, and
  emotional context. Do not map every you to คุณ, เธอ, นาย, or ท่าน.
- Keep each character's voice distinct. Do not make all characters use the
  same polite register.
- Preserve meaningful titles and roles, but do not add Japanese honorifics
  that are absent from the English source.
- Internal thought should remain clearly attributable to the viewpoint
  character. Do not turn thoughts into omniscient narration.

## Translation and formatting

- Translate English prose to Thai only; do not leave source English
  parentheticals after approved Thai terms.
- Preserve dialogue, internal thought, scene breaks, sound effects, and
  system-like text when they carry story meaning.
- Do not invent canon explanations, powers, chronology, or relationship facts.
- Keep chapter numbers in Arabic digits and use Thai titles for future output.
