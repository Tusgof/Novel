## Category Guidance

For this Re:Zero fanfiction, add only recurring canon or story-specific terms that prevent
real consistency errors. Preferred categories:

- `character` — named canon or fanfiction characters
- `location` — kingdoms, cities, estates, sanctuaries, and named sites
- `ability` — named authorities, blessings, curses, and recurring powers
- `entity` — spirits, witches, divine beasts, and named supernatural beings
- `organization` — camps, cults, orders, and factions
- `item` — named weapons, artifacts, insignia, and recurring objects
- `concept` — canon concepts whose established Thai rendering must stay stable
- `title` — ranks, positions, epithets

Avoid generic words such as magic, knight, death, mansion, or spirit unless they are part of
a recurring proper name or canon concept.

## Runtime Contract

- `status = proposed` means the term still needs human approval.
- `status = approved` means the runtime can treat the term as canonical.
- `status = deprecated` means the term should stay searchable but should not be used for new translations.
- `aliases` should list source spellings or variant forms that the scanner may match.
- `first_seen_chapter` and `first_seen_block` record where the term was first discovered in the source text.
