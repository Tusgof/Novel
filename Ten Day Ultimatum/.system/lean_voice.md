# Ten Day Ultimatum

Source: Chinese web novel `十日终焉` by `杀虫队队员`.
Genre: mystery, survival thriller, speculative fantasy.

Use natural, tense Thai prose suited to a closed-room survival mystery. Preserve
the source's uncertainty, reveals, pacing, humour, names, dialogue and scene
beats. Let relationships and the immediate scene determine pronouns; do not
inherit another novel's character policy. Keep chapter titles as Thai reader
titles and preserve Arabic chapter numbers. Do not invent explanations for
unfamiliar terms; keep a candidate for later glossary review instead.
