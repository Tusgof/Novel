# IRS Glossary Approval Decision: ch071-ch080

## Decision

- Candidates reviewed: 171
- New approvals: none
- Rejected as generic nouns, real-world references, OCR/mixed-language noise, title fragments, or variants of established terms: all candidates

## Rationale

This source range contains heavy mixed-language and OCR-style noise. It also repeats established concepts such as Library Society, Great Library, Undertaker, Tutorial Fairy, and Dang Seo-rin in variants with articles or malformed combined phrases. Adding 171 new glossary notes would reduce, rather than improve, precision. Existing approved glossary notes and Sentinel coverage remain the gate for canonical terms.
