## Category Guidance

For this novel (Deep Sea Embers), use categories appropriate for a nautical dark fantasy,
NOT wuxia/xianxia/cultivation fiction. Preferred categories:

- `character` — people, crew members, captains, named individuals
- `location` — ports, islands, ruins, ships, underwater sites, fog zones
- `vessel` — ships, boats, ghost vessels, wrecks
- `entity` — supernatural beings, specters, anomalies, creatures
- `organization` — crews, fleets, orders, guilds (NOT sects or cultivation schools)
- `item` — tools, artifacts, navigational instruments, relics
- `phenomenon` — fog, tides, curses, distortions, unnatural events
- `title` — ranks, positions, epithets

Avoid: `sect`, `realm`, `technique`, `cultivation_stage`, `martial_art`.
