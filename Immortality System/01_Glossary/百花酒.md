## Category Guidance

For this novel, prefer categories that distinguish:

- `character`: named people and recurring aliases
- `location`: cities, villages, ruins, mountains, and named regions
- `sect`: sects, clans, schools, and factions
- `realm`: cultivation levels, realms, and domains
- `technique`: cultivation methods, spells, and martial techniques
- `artifact`: named weapons, treasures, pills, and tools
- `title`: ranks, honorifics, and official positions
- `term`: recurring concepts and system labels

Avoid creating glossary entries for ordinary verbs, generic nouns, one-off descriptions,
or a full sentence.

## Runtime Contract

- `status = proposed` means the term still needs human approval.
- `status = approved` means the runtime can treat the term as canonical.
- `status = deprecated` means the term stays searchable but should not be used for new translations.
- `aliases` lists source spellings or variants that the scanner may match.
- `first_seen_chapter` and `first_seen_block` record where the term was first discovered.
